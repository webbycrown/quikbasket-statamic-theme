## Pictures

The `.svg` files under `public/assets/image` are simple interface icons (search, cart, shipping, and the other small marks). No brand logo is in them. `main-white-logo.webp` is the Quikbasket wordmark, `parcel pack.webp` is a line icon, `usa flage.webp` is a flag, and `offer-bg.webp` is a plain background. `payments-cards-2.svg` is a decorative footer graphic; the kit does not process cards.

The other `.webp` photographs are original product stills made for Quikbasket: shirts, knitwear, denim, dresses, coats, shoes, bags, scarves, hats, and a chain. No person is in them. No brand name is printed on them. WebbyCrown holds the copyright and may redistribute them with this kit.

The kit does not include a video file.
